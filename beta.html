Setup

Install DNS Profile

How to install
  1. Open this page on your iPhone or iPad in Safari.
  2. Pick any certificate above and tap Install, then confirm the prompt.
  3. Wait for the app to finish installing on your Home Screen.
  4. Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the developer profile, and Trust it.
  5. Launch the app. If one certificate is revoked or won't open, just install with another.
3

Completion

You're all set.

That's it. Open SwiftInstaller from your Home Screen and use it to install SwiftStore (or SwiftStore + SwiftContainer), straight from your device. No PC needed.

Once SwiftStore is installed, the DNS profile is no longer needed. Remove it in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → SwiftInstaller → Remove Profile — or keep it and just stop using its DNS by switching DNS back to Automatic in the same menu.
